[253] Note 62. Chapter 7 - 7.3 Threats to Validity
[254] Reviewer concern: Threats to validity đã có nhưng là một đoạn dài. Nên tách theo internal/external/construct/conclusion validity để đúng văn phong nghiên cứu và dễ đọc.
[255] Suggested rewrite / fix:
[256] The evaluation has several threats to validity. Internal validity may be affected by implementation differences between TKNT and external baselines, because baseline outputs must be converted into the common scoring schema. External validity is limited by the benchmark scope: the tested cases focus on living rooms and bedrooms and do not cover kitchens, bathrooms, offices, or construction-level design tasks. Construct validity is limited by the metrics themselves; geometry, request following, and image-fidelity scores capture important properties but cannot fully represent human design preference or professional interior-design quality. Conclusion validity is limited by the number of benchmark cases and repeated runs, especially for provider-based image generation where outputs may vary across calls. For these reasons, the reported results should be interpreted as evidence that the proposed workflow improves controllability in the tested setting, not as proof that it solves all interior-design tasks.
